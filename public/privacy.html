You may stop receiving SMS text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To resume messages, reply START. For help, reply HELP or contact us at charlie.cessex@gmail.com.

Opting out of SMS messages does not delete previously collected information. To request deletion of your personal data, email us at charlie.cessex@gmail.com.
